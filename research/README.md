# Research retrieval records

The three retrieval.json files record primary-source URLs, content hashes, retrieval results and access failures used during the 3.1 research. They are research provenance, not runtime dependencies or proof of listening quality. Third-party partial source snapshots are not redistributed here; retrieve the cited revision and verify its hash. Runtime dependency sources and licenses are included separately in the corresponding source package. The implementation/limitations are documented in docs/SMOOTHER-RESEARCH-3.1.md and related research notes.
